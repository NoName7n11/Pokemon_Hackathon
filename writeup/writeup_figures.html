Win rate falls monotonically as search widens

Bounded UCT replacing the 1-ply lookahead, four configurations, 200 games each against the accepted heuristic baseline. Monotonic decline in search width is the signature of a weak evaluator, not weak search: a wider root gives UCT more ways to exploit the position evaluator's blind spots.

Zero faults across all arms; latency was never the constraint (9.2 ms mean, 97 ms max). An earlier attempt was voided by a wiring bug that bypassed the per-turn ability cap — all four arms above are the cap-fixed measurements.

Figure 2

Two significant results that did not survive more games

Both candidates cleared a nominal significance threshold at their first look and collapsed toward parity when the sample grew. Screening, main, confirmation and extension stages are four chances for noise to cross a threshold — and one of them did. A pre-committed 55% effect-size bar, not a p-value gate, is what kept these out of the agent.

Figure 3

Nine mechanisms tested, one accepted

Every mechanism was proven to change decisions before games were spent on it, then measured against a frozen baseline. The one that cleared the bar fixed a context the baseline answered arbitrarily; the ones that merely improved a ranking it already computed all landed inside the noise floor.
