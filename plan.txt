THE ACTUAL RUBRIC (this is what gets scored — everything below maps to it)
======================================================================
MODEL SCORE — 70%
  - clarity of approach + rationale explained
  - originality / technical soundness
  - consistency under repeated matches / stable conditions
  - avoids over-reliance on specific initial states, matchups, situational
    advantages
  - performance within the competition track (leaderboard) — ONE factor
    among the above, explicitly NOT the whole score

DECK SCORE — 20%
  - clarity of deck concept + alignment with strategy
  - effective key-card selection/utilization

REPORT SCORE — 10%
  - logical structure / writing clarity
  - effective use of figures / charts / tables

Constraints: title + subtitle (separate fields) + must select Track (only
"Main Track" exists) + body <=2000 words (overage may be penalized) + Media
Gallery is a SEPARATE optional attachment (images/video), distinct from
anything embedded in the body. No Kaggle-mandated section template beyond
"title, subtitle, detailed analysis."

2. EVIDENCE OF RIGOR  (~500 words) — Model: originality/soundness
  Three hypothesis -> test -> result blocks, not narrative:
  a) __file__/exec() bug: two failed Kaggle submissions, root-caused via a

     re-fails identically, new code passes). Source: PROGRESS.md 2026-08-08.

     identical to ours by card name; head-to-head under our own agent is a

     Source: PROGRESS.md Dipam scouting entry, this session's Decs/ compare.

     damage=0; our agent ranks by that field, so it mis-scores 24% of all
     engine attacks (381/1556). Proved with a one-line patch: 20% -> 37%
     win rate, zero deck changes. Source: PROGRESS.md Gardevoir agent-arm
     entry (stock/dmg/bench/full table).
